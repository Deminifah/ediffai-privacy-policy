Delete Your EDIFFAI Academy Account

Users of EDIFFAI Academy may request deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time.

Email: info@ediffai.com

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to ediffai@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the phone number and name used in the application.

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Name
  • Phone Number
  • User Profile Information
  • Learning Records Associated With Your Account

Processing Time

Deletion requests are processed within 14 business days.

Data Retention

Certain records may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or regulatory purposes.
